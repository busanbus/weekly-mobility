Seoul

City Bus Accidents Have Surged 40% in Two Years

The number of accidents involving Seoul city buses increased by approximately 40 percent, from 2,343 in 2023 to 3,279 in 2025, and the Seoul Metropolitan Council called for the implementation of joint safety measures, including enhanced safety training for drivers and inspections of accident-prone routes and sections.

Seoul

Increase in Suspected Cases of Intentional Fare Evasion by Foreign Nationals

As there have been a series of incidents on Seoul city buses where foreign tourists have failed to pay their fares even after being notified that their transit card balance was insufficient, calls are growing for the provision of multilingual fare notices and the development of response guidelines for bus drivers.

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport

Notice of Special Exemptions for the Mobility Regulatory Sandbox Pilot Program

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport has announced regulatory exemptions for electric vehicle battery subscription services and plug-in hydrogen hybrid buses to reduce initial purchase costs, facilitate battery recovery and recycling, and validate eco-friendly bus technologies.

Gimhae

Designation of a Training Center for Private Bus Drivers

To address the chronic shortage of city bus drivers, Gimhae City plans to designate private training institutions as training centers and expand its system for securing a stable workforce by providing on-the-job training and facilitating employment with local transportation companies.
