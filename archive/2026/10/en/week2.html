Gyeonggi

Challenges in Securing Additional Funding to Maintain and Expand the Public Management System for City Buses

Gyeonggi Province had planned to transition 6,300 city buses to a public management system by 2027, but due to financial burdens and budget cuts, it is considering slowing the pace of the transition and plans to alleviate the financial burden by reducing operating costs and increasing advertising revenue.

Ulsan

Survey on City Bus Route Restructuring: “Shorter Intervals Between Buses” Top the List of Requests

In a survey on the reorganization of Ulsan City’s bus routes, the most common request was to shorten the intervals between buses. The city plans to resume service on some previously discontinued routes and, following a consulting study on the reorganization, implement the route changes in the second half of 2027.
