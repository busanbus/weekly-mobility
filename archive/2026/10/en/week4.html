Seoul

“When the bus stops → When the rear door opens”: Change to bus exit announcement

The Seoul Metropolitan Government plans to change the announcement prompting passengers to disembark to “Please disembark slowly when the rear doors open,” in response to a suggestion from a citizen, to prevent safety accidents caused by passengers moving too early before the bus comes to a complete stop.

Seoul

Plan to Create a Linear Park Along National Assembly Boulevard

The Seoul Metropolitan Government plans to construct a 4.1-kilometer underground tunnel on Gukhoe-daero and a linear park covering approximately 110,000 square meters above it, and will implement traffic management measures—such as converting the road into a seven-lane at-grade intersection—during the construction period.

Seoul

Cheonggyecheon Autonomous Shuttle: Plans to Launch Nighttime Service and Gradually Introduce Fares

The Seoul Metropolitan Government plans to launch the nation’s first regular nighttime service of driverless, steering-wheel-less autonomous buses to connect downtown tourist attractions with local commercial districts, and to establish the service as a regular public transportation option by transitioning to a paid system in phases following an initial free trial period.

Ranked No. 1 in the World for AI Adoption and Utilization Capabilities

The Seoul Metropolitan Government ranked third overall and first in the world in the category of AI adoption and utilization in The Observer’s 2026 AI City Index, and is applying AI technology to the transportation sector as well, including autonomous buses and AI-based flood risk prediction.
