Reform of the Semi-Public Operation System for City Buses

Changwon City plans to save approximately 10 billion won annually by converting some low-revenue bus routes to village buses and demand-responsive transit (DRT), expanding its bus advertising business, reducing fuel costs, selling carbon credits, and taking over the operation of electric bus charging facilities.
