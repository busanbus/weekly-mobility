South Gyeongsang Province

Busan-Gimhae Light Rail Introduces T-Money Tagless Payment System

The Busan-Gimhae Light Rail plans to install the T-Money contactless payment system at all 21 stations by February 2027—the first such system in the South Gyeongsang region—and officially open the line at the end of March; however, if the payment system cannot be integrated with that of the Busan Metropolitan Subway, inconvenience is expected for transfer passengers.

First Test Vehicles Deployed in Gwangju’s Autonomous Driving Demonstration City

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport plans to begin operating the first demonstration vehicles in the Gwangju Autonomous Driving Demonstration City on November 30 and to deploy approximately 120 autonomous vehicles on public roads by the end of the year to create a safe demonstration environment.

Korail

KTX Trains Set to Be Retired: Commercialization of Project to Convert Them into High-Speed Freight Trains Under Review

Korail is considering a plan to convert its early-generation KTX trains, which are nearing the end of their service life, into freight-only trains. The company is seeking to increase the share of rail freight transport through high-value-added freight services and the introduction of refrigerated and frozen containers, while also exploring opportunities to enter new logistics businesses.
