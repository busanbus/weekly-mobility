Driverless Autonomous Driving: Still a Long Way to Go

Demand-responsive transit (DRT) and autonomous taxis have the potential to evolve into driverless transportation services; however, since they currently require the involvement of drivers and safety officers, operational systems—including dispatch, remote support, and emergency stops—need to be further refined to enable fully driverless commercialization.

"Firefly Bus" Service Launched to Connect Major Downtown Areas Late at Night

Starting November 27, the Seoul Metropolitan Government will operate the “Firefly Bus,” a late-night express bus service that quickly connects major downtown hubs such as Hongdae, Gwanghwamun, Myeongdong, Seoul Station, and Gangnam Station, with the aim of improving late-night transportation and revitalizing nighttime commercial districts.
