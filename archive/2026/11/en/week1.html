Ulsan

Study to Assess the Feasibility of Introducing a Publicly Operated City Bus System Underway

The City of Ulsan announced in a bid notice on November 2 that it will commission a 14-month feasibility study, with a budget of 500 million won, to examine the introduction of a public operation system. The city plans to review implementation strategies for the transition, including utilizing the Ulsan Urban Development Corporation and establishing the Ulsan Transportation Corporation.

To prevent the decline of rural areas, we must first address mobility issues

It has been pointed out that residents in rural and agricultural areas are facing restrictions on their right to mobility due to a decline in bus ridership and longer intervals between bus trips, and that expanding public transportation infrastructure and ensuring the right to mobility must come first in order to bridge the urban-rural transportation gap.
