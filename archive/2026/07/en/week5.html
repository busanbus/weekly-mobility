Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport

Finalization and Announcement of the BRT Comprehensive Plan (2026–2030)

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport’s Metropolitan Area Transport Committee has finalized and announced the Second Comprehensive Plan for the Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) System for 2026–2030, and stated that it will double the length of dedicated BRT lanes nationwide by 2030 to increase bus travel speeds by 20%.

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, and Transport

Winning Entries from the Public Idea Contest Directly Translated into Policy

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure and Transport announced that it has selected five winning entries from a total of 417 proposals submitted to the “Public Idea Contest for Land, Infrastructure, and Transport,” and that it will implement policy improvements—such as amendments to laws and regulations, regulatory exemptions, and administrative measures—based on these proposals.

Seoul

Establishing Standards for Multi-Story Roads to Improve Land Use Efficiency

The Seoul Metropolitan Government has established and is implementing the “Standards for Three-Dimensional Planning of Urban Planning Facilities (Roads)” to allow for the installation of roads and transportation facilities on portions of privately-owned land, while utilizing the space beneath them for parking lots and other purposes.

Hyundai Motor Unveils Its First Corporate Social Responsibility Project, Donating Vehicles Designed for Expectant Mothers to Regions with Low Birth Rates

Hyundai Motor Company donated vehicles designed specifically for pregnant women as part of its corporate social responsibility project, “Hyundai Universal Solution,” aimed at enhancing the mobility rights of people with limited mobility and improving their access to medical care and welfare services.
