Hyundai to Supply Tens of Thousands of Robo-Taxis to Waymo

Starting in October, Hyundai Motor will begin mass-producing and supplying tens of thousands of Ioniq 5 robotaxis for Waymo at its plant in Georgia, marking the full-scale launch of its foundry business, in which it manufactures vehicles exclusively for autonomous driving companies.

Public Opinion Survey on Designating City Buses as Essential Public Services

In a survey of 1,000 adults nationwide, 78.3% responded that city buses should be designated as essential public services under the Labor Union Act; however, the Ministry of Employment and Labor and labor groups have expressed caution or opposition due to concerns about restrictions on labor rights.
