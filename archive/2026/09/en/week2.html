Seoul Robo-Taxi Hailing Service Platform: Hyundai Motor and T-Money Join, Breaking KakaoT's Monopoly

The Seoul Metropolitan Government has selected Kakao Mobility, Hyundai Motor Company, and T-Money Mobility as private-sector operators for its autonomous vehicle transportation platform.
