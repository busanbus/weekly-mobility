Ulsan

Pushing for the Transition to a Publicly Operated City Bus System

The city of Ulsan is beginning a full-scale review of switching to a publicly operated city bus system in order to address the limitations of the current privatized system—which restricts the city’s authority to adjust routes and schedules despite the annual increase in financial support for bus operations.
