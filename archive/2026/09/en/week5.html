National Assembly

Ruling Party Pushes for 10-Year Limit on Bus Operating Licenses

Rep. Kim Nam-geun of the Democratic Party of Korea introduced a bill to amend the Passenger Motor Vehicle Transportation Business Act, which would limit the validity period of route bus operating licenses to a maximum of 10 years and require local governments to select new operators through competitive bidding upon expiration.

Korea National Railroad Corporation

Project to Utilize Underutilized Railway Land: Plans for the Construction of the Gijang Ocean Blue Rail

The Korea National Railroad Corporation has selected eight projects nationwide to utilize idle land—including the Gijang Ocean Blue Rail—and will invest 57.8 billion won starting at the end of this year. For the Gijang project, the corporation plans to invest 47.9 billion won to develop tourism infrastructure on the abandoned Donghae Nambu Line site and in the Wolnae Port area, with completion targeted for 2028.
